Morning Updates

Two free daily shows to listen to on the move — on the way to work, school, or back from drop-off. New episodes every weekday morning (Taipei time).

Tech Unzip

A 30-minute daily tech briefing for your walk, commute or school run. Hosts Ryan and Heart cover the day's biggest stories in AI, big data, chips, e-commerce and product design, then unzip them: what to remember, how it changes society and work, and one concept explained simply. New every weekday, never repeating what you've already heard.
